01 — The Unbudgeted Line Item

Law is the last profession
that bills by the mystery.

Every other corporate function plans its spend. Marketing has budgets. IT has budgets. Procurement negotiates every contract to the decimal. Then the legal invoice arrives — six figures, lightly itemized, essentially take it or leave it.

Corporate legal departments now manage some of the largest discretionary budgets in the enterprise, yet matter-level budgeting remains rare, inconsistent, and often adversarial. The gap between what litigation costs and what it should cost is where fortunes quietly leak.

LitigationBudgeting.com names the discipline closing that gap — the software, the methodology, and the professional standard that turns legal spend from invoice shock into forecast.
